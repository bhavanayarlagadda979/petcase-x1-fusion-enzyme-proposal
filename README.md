# Hybrid PETase–Cutinase Fusion Enzyme Proposal (PETCase-X1)

A research proposal exploring a hypothetical trifunctional fusion enzyme — combining a PETase catalytic domain, a thermostable cutinase domain, and a biosurfactant-binding linker — for enhanced enzymatic biodegradation of PET and related polyesters.

## Contents

- [`hybrid_enzyme_PET_biodegradation_proposal.md`](./hybrid_enzyme_PET_biodegradation_proposal.md) — full proposal: abstract, introduction, materials, methodology/procedure, projected results, and discussion.

## Status

📝 **Proposal / hypothesis document.** No wet-lab or computational experiments have been carried out yet. The "Expected Results" section explicitly describes projected outcomes inferred from trends in the cited literature, not measured data. This is a planning document for possible future experimental work, not a completed study.

## Background reading

This proposal synthesizes themes from the following literature on enzymatic plastic/pollutant biodegradation:

- Hernández-Nava et al. (2025), *World Journal of Microbiology and Biotechnology* — PET biodegradation by microorganisms and enzymes (experimental + in silico review)
- Watts, Khoba & Purty (2023), *Bioremediation Journal* — in silico evaluation of MHETase degradation efficiency
- Mousavi et al. (2021), *Biochemistry Research International* — recent advances in bioremediation enzymes
- Charupanit et al. (2022), *Molecules* — in silico identification of plastic-degrading enzyme active sites via MD
- Additional sources referenced but paywalled at time of writing (see References section in the proposal)
